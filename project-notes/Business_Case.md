## 1. The idea
The primary customer are people who enjoy music, including musicians, singers, songwriters and music fans. The app is ,made for people who want to connect with other music lovers, find people to play with, collaborate on music, helps others and discover new music and artists.

Today, it can be hard to find and connect with other people who share the same passion for music. A lot of people struggle to find people to play with, form bands, collaborate on songs and discover music events. Currently, people like myself often use social media, posters, group chats, or word of mouth, which is hard to reach a large audience and find the right people.

This website brings music lovers together all in one place, making a community specifically for people who enjoy music. When signing up, users create profiles listing their instruments, favorite genres, skill level and music interests. The app uses this information to match people with similar interests. Users can find bandmates, collaborate on songs, share music, improve their skills and connect discover music events and artists. Unlike what people use now like social media, everything on the app is focused on music, making it easier to find the right people and build music communities.

The main sources of revenue will be from allowing artists to pay to promote a new album, upcoming concert and other music releases. Music venues will also be able to promote opportunities for musicians to promote at their locations. We also offer memberships with additional features, such as the ability to create your own music community within the app(for example jazz lovers community).

## 2. Where IT creates value
The website creates value through data and differentiation. Data plays a very important role with our website because when users create their profiles, they provide information about their instrument, favorite genres, skill levels and music interests. The app uses this information to match users with similar interests. This makes finding people to jam with, collaborate with and find new music friends much easier. Over time as more users join the website, it will have more users to matfh with and uses this information to improve its recommendations and provide better results. 

Differentiation is another important concept because our app offers something different from the general social media platforms people currently use. Instead of being on a general social network with all sorts of people and communities, it is specifically designed for music lovers. Users can find similar musicians, collaborate, share music and discover local events all in one place. This makes it so much easier to create the community that music has lost and connect with people who share the same music passion.



## 3. Network effects


The website does become more valuable as more people actively use it. This is because the websites is built around connecting music lovers and creating new opportunities that people didn’t have before. The more users on the app, the more people there are to connect with, play together share music and learn.

The website is a two-sided market because it brings two main groups together. The first side is music lovers such as musicians, signers, songwriters and people who want to play and meet other music lovers. The second side is local artist, bands and venues who wants to promote their albums, songs and performance opportunities. Both sides benefit from each other because the more people there is on one side create more opportunities for the other, overall making the app become more valuable.

As more people join and the network grows, the extra value come from having more people and opportunities available for both sides. For example, a guitarist like myslef wants to find other people to play. The more users, the better chance I have to find someone. More music fans also create a larger audience for artists and venues to promote their events. At the same time more artists and venues give users more music and events to look at. This creates a cycle where each side makes the app more valuable for the other.

To get people using the app in the beginning, we would connect with the local universities and ask them to help promote it through campus events, posters and the university Social media pages. We would also promote it on social media pages such as Instagram, Facebook, TikTok because this is where most of the music lovers go to now to connect but its not a dedicated space for music. These would help attract our first users and starts building that music community.

The prototype demonstrates this network effect with live counters showing how many music lovers are using the app, along with the number of collaborations, communities and events. These counts update as the community grows, making the activity and opportunities on the platform visible to users.
